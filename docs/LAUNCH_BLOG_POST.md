# Poetik is Live on the Play Store

Today I'm launching **Poetik** — a mobile app for reading, writing, and learning poetry.

## Why Poetik?

Poetry shouldn't feel intimidating. Poetik combines a community feed of classic and user-written poems, a guided writing studio with form templates, and interactive lessons that reward you with XP and streaks.

## What You Can Do

- **Read** poems by theme, era, and form
- **Write** with haiku, sonnet, and free-verse templates
- **Learn** through lessons, quizzes, and daily challenges
- **Connect** with fellow poets via messages and playlists

## Built With

React Native, Expo, Supabase, and a lot of late nights.
