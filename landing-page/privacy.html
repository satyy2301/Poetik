Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-17

Overview

Poetik ("we", "our", "the app") is a poetry learning and writing application. This policy describes what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

  • Account information: email address, display name, profile data you provide at signup.
  • Content: poems, drafts, messages, and learning progress you create in the app.
  • Usage data: screens visited, lessons completed, and feature interactions (analytics).
  • Device data: app version, platform, and crash reports for stability.

How We Use Data

  • Provide core features (reading, writing, learning, messaging).
  • Personalize your experience and track XP, streaks, and achievements.
  • Moderate content and prevent abuse.
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs.

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase — authentication, database, and storage.
  • Sentry (optional) — error monitoring when enabled.
  • PostHog (optional) — product analytics when enabled.

Data Retention

We retain your account data while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting support.

Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at privacy@poetik.app.

Children's Privacy

Poetik is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions: privacy@poetik.app
