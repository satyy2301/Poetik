# Play Store Listing — Poetik

## Short Description (80 chars max)

Read, write, and learn poetry. Build streaks, earn XP, join a community of poets.

## Full Description

Poetik is your pocket companion for poetry — read classic and community poems, write your own with guided templates, and level up through interactive lessons and quizzes.

**Read** — Browse poems by theme, era, and form. Follow poets you love and discover new voices.

**Write** — Draft with haiku, sonnet, and free-verse templates. Auto-save keeps your work safe.

**Learn** — Complete lessons, take quizzes, and tackle daily challenges to earn XP and achievements.

**Connect** — Message fellow poets, share playlists, and stay motivated with streaks and leaderboards.

Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned writer, Poetik helps you grow as a poet every day.
